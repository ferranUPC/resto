Evaluation budget

For each suite, start from a preset (minimum, planned, extended) or set your own: how many inputs to run in each pass, which capability levels to test, how many models per level and how many repetitions. The bounds come from the cost data. Totals are low to high, in USD, with V1 and V2 apart and combined against the cap. The selection is kept in the page address: copy the link to share exactly this choice.
