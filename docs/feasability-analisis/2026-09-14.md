## Pace vs. the calendar

Work plan §0 budgets E0 at 70 h over 9–18 Sep (10 calendar days). Landing all eight tasks by 14 Sep is
comfortably ahead of that budget — the first milestone came in early rather than late, which is the
opposite of what an already-overcommitted plan (+6%, zero contingency) usually produces at its first
checkpoint. That's a genuinely good sign, but one data point: E0 is also the epic with the most
design-only work (dataclasses, contracts, docs) and the least SUMO-in-the-loop uncertainty apart from
E0.5/E0.6, which themselves went smoothly. E1 is a different kind of epic — two MCP servers, `traci_api`
with SUMO in the loop, and a from-scratch SQLite backend — and nothing in it has started: every file
under `application/tools/`, `adapters/sumo/` (`traci_api.py`, `netconvert.py`, `plain_edit.py`, writers)
and `interface/mcp/` is still a 1–5 line placeholder. E1 is budgeted 94 h over 21 Sep–2 Oct (12 calendar
days, ~9 working days at 40h/week pace) — a noticeably tighter ratio than E0's, and the first epic where
being ahead on paper doesn't yet tell us anything about velocity on agent/tool code, which is what most
of the rest of the plan is made of.

## Risks observed this review

1. **`tfm-work-plan.md` E0.2/E1.3 still say "Postgres + `pgvector`"** two commits after the architecture
   froze on SQLite (ADR-0012). Purely cosmetic today, but the plan is the document the Friday ritual
   (§6) is run against — leaving it stale invites a future review to either re-flag E0.2 as open or, worse,
   read E1.3 against the wrong backend. Cheap fix, low urgency, but it's the one open item from
   yesterday's review that didn't get closed today.
2. **`docs/DATABASE_MCP_CONTRACT.md`'s own title still reads "v1.0-draft"** even though its status line
   and the architecture freeze both call it done. Same category as #1: no functional effect, just a
   document that hasn't been told the milestone it belongs to already landed.
3. **E1 is a step up in kind, not just size, from E0.** Foundations was largely typed contracts and one
   well-scoped SUMO network; E1 is two MCP servers, `traci_api` with SUMO in the loop, and a SQLite
   backend with a conformance suite, all currently at zero. Being 4 days ahead on E0 is worth nothing if
   it isn't followed by real velocity on E1.1 next week — that's the number worth checking at the next
   review, not the cumulative percentage.
