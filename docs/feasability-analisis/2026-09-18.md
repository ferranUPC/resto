## Risks and observations from this review

1. **Tracker-maintenance risk, now mitigated but worth naming plainly**: an interactive tracker
   update can land code, write accurate prose about it in another task's Notes, and still forget to
   flip the task's own status cell. This isn't a one-off — it happened to three tasks in the same
   sitting. The `SKILL.md` fix (cross-check every row against the repo, not just the recent diff)
   is a direct countermeasure; whether it's sufficient will show in the next review that catches
   (or doesn't catch) a similar gap.
2. **The E4.7 funding block is unchanged and still small enough to resolve immediately.** $2.90
   gating a "must not cut" milestone (work plan §5) for two days running is disproportionate to the
   sum — see every review since 17 Sep for the same point.
3. **E2.5 (Runner online mode) and E8 (thesis writing, still 0/8) are unchanged flags** from prior
   reviews — not urgent yet, still worth tracking.

## Recommendation

**Keep going as planned.** The one concrete action worth taking outside the engineering plan

finding — that a routine interactive update silently under-recorded three finished tasks — is worth
