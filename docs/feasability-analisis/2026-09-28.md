prerequisites, not the milestones' own deliverables.** E3.8 and E5.13 are the kind of task that unblocks
three chains at once but produces zero visible movement on E4.2–E4.4, E3.7, E5.2 — the actual milestone-

percentage-done number will likely look flat or move slowly for the next stretch even while real,
necessary unblocking work happens, and then jump when E3.7/E4.2–E4.4 land. Worth remembering when reading
next week's number so a flat percentage isn't mistaken for stalling.

weekend is a normal rhythm — but if the next review (in a few days) still shows no movement on E3.7 or
E4.2–E4.4, that would be worth a closer look, since both are now fully unblocked and sit on multiple
critical chains at once.
