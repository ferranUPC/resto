## Pace vs. the v0.3 calendar

Wave 1a alone landed 68 of 954 plan points in four days, against a nominal capacity of 38.5 pts/week
(about 5.5 pts/day). At face value that is more than double the planned rate, but I read it the same way
v0.3's own preamble reads the team's history: "ran well ahead of v0.2's calendar," not as a new baseline
to extrapolate from. Wave 1a was front-loaded with mechanical, well-specified work: a data migration
with a known target shape, an ADR acceptance with a scoped fix, a benchmark sweep against already-built
tooling. Wave 1b's remaining items (E3.7's plan bank, E3.9's budget document, E3.10's log trim) and
especially E5.2/E5.4 (the Coordinator and Composer agents, still 4-line placeholders) are a different
kind of work: building new agents from scratch rather than migrating and measuring existing ones. I
would not bet the calendar on a repeat of this exact rate, but I also see no reason to discount the
underlying signal. This team clears well-scoped build tasks fast, and the plan's +170-point slack was
sized with exactly that history in mind.

## Bottleneck observed this review

Last review's flagged risk, that a domain-definition decision (ADR-0027's acceptance) could take
"multiple days of iteration" the way the `intent` convention did, did not happen. E5.13 was decided and
pushed the same week it was scoped, with a real bug fix included, not just a stamp of approval. That is
good news for the Coordinator spine. The one bottleneck I can point to concretely this review is
different: the diagnostic family's zero-margin Jaccard. Three prompt iterations (v2, v3, v4) were needed
to get from a typed-cause feature to a passing score, and the version that passes does so at exactly
0.60, one wrong edge away from failing outright in Validation 2. The maintainer's own four-item lever
list (trace the hidden reasoning, cap it separately from the output budget, shorten the tool chain, or
nudge after a cut step) is already written down and estimated at about $0.35 to try, cheap insurance that
has been deliberately deferred rather than dropped. Whether that deferral was right is a call I'd only
revisit if EXP-01 actually comes in under 0.60; right now it reads as a reasonable trade against a fixed
evaluation budget.

## Recommendation

**Keep going as planned.** M2 is done ahead of schedule, the test suite and static checks are clean, and
the one real technical risk this review surfaced (E4.3's zero-margin diagnostic Jaccard) already has a
costed, written-down mitigation plan that the maintainer chose not to spend yet. That is a considered
trade, not an oversight, and no step of the work-plan §5 fallback order is warranted over it. The
concrete action for next week is to start wave 1b's Coordinator-spine work (E3.7, then E5.2) now that
both of its blockers are cleared, since that chain has had no forward motion of its own this review
despite everything around it moving.
