## Risks observed this review

1. **Two days of the project's best work exist only on one laptop.** Nothing in the drafts, schemas,
   contract or amendment is committed, pushed or CI-verified. A disk failure or an accidental
   `git checkout .` would erase most of E0.3 and E0.4. This review deliberately commits only its own two
   files and leaves that work untouched — but it should be committed today.
2. **Scope change recorded in one doc but not the plan.** A1 re-scopes E0.2 and E1.3 in the architecture
   doc; `tfm-work-plan.md` still says Postgres + `pgvector`, and the tracker can't close E0.2 honestly
   until the plan agrees. Minor, but it's the kind of drift that makes later reviews ambiguous.
3. **E0.4 draft has three open points that touch the domain.** Contract §10.1 (`find_network(name=…)` vs
   a `Network` with no name field) is a domain decision, and §10.2 (embedding ownership) affects whether
   E4.9's learning-effect numbers are comparable — the M5 thesis result. Neither blocks M0, but §10.2
   should be decided before E1.4, not discovered during E4.9.
4. **No SUMO artefact yet, 5 days before a milestone that needs three running demand profiles.** Everything
   so far is pure Python; the first contact with `netgenerate`/`duarouter`/congestion tuning is where
   estimates typically break.
